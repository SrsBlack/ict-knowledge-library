## How to Use

- **First read:** Track 1 in order, no skipping.
- **Working trader review:** Track 2 once Track 1 feels stale.
- **Catching up to current ICT:** Track 3, especially sections 3.5–3.6.
- **Reference mode:** ignore the tracks; jump from `INDEX.md` to whichever file you need.

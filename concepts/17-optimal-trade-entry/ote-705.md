## Definition

The OTE 0.705 entry is the **canonical optimal entry** — the mid-point of the OTE zone (between 0.62 and 0.79). ICT teaches it as the highest-conviction entry depth when paired with PD-array confluence.

**ICT names it the "sweet spot" twice, and defines it arithmetically as the midpoint of the other two levels:** "we trade down into the **70.5 level, which is the sweet spot by my definition** for optimal trade entry, and you could be a buyer there" (`ICT-2020-OTE-VOL03`, 04:28); "it takes us right up in the **70.5 sweet spot**" (`ICT-2017-OTE-DRILL-USDJPY`, 02:37). The construction: "maybe 70.5, which is **the midway point between the 79 and 62 % retracement**" (`ICT-2021-OTE-PRICE-ACTION-LESSON`, 04:36).

⚠ **Sweet spot ≠ default execution depth.** The same corpus that names 0.705 the sweet spot prescribes **0.62** as the entry to actually place, on fill-probability and spread grounds — "don't try to do the 70.5, don't look for the 79, use 62" (`ICT-2021-OTE-SCALPING-EXERCISE`, 13:32). 0.705 is the geometric and conviction centre; 0.62 is the executable one. See [ote-62](ote-62.md). This page previously called 0.705 "the practical default OTE depth", which the applied material contradicts.

## Formal Criteria

- Retracement reaches 0.705 of measured leg.
- PD array at or near 0.705.
- HTF bias agreement.
- **SL at the leg-origin extreme (fib 1.0)** — ⚠ *corrected 2026-08-05; previously "beyond 0.79". See [ote-overview](ote-overview.md).*

## Formula / Math

```
OTE_705_entry = leg_end - 0.705 * leg_size
SL            = leg_start                     # fib 1.0, exactly

# Bullish leg 1.0800 → 1.0900:
OTE_705_entry = 1.08295
SL            = 1.0800
Risk          = 1.08295 - 1.0800 = 29.5 pips
# first target 1.0900 (fib 0.0) = 70.5 pips ≈ 2.4R — clears the Primer's 2:1 floor.
```

## Examples

**Example 1 — H1 0.705 entry (canonical):**
- Leg 1.0800 → 1.0900.
- 0.705 = 1.08295; bullish FVG at 1.0828–1.0832.
- Long at 1.0830, **SL 1.0800 — the leg origin, fib 1.0.** Risk = 29.5 pips.
- First target 1.0900 (fib 0.0) ≈ 2.4R; the SD ladder extends beyond it.
- ⚠ *Corrected 2026-08-11: previously "SL 1.0815, risk 15 pips", a 0.79-based stop — the placement the 2026-08-05 correction removed from the Formal Criteria above. The page had been half-corrected.*

## Common Mistakes

- **Pixel-precision.** Use buffer of ±0.5–1 pip; don't insist on exact 1.08295.
- **Ignoring PD-array.** 0.705 alone without an FVG/OB at the level is just a fib line.
- **Calling 0.618 "OTE 0.705."** Classical 0.618 is NOT 0.705; they differ by ~9%.

## Definition

This file documents **ICT's public commentary on Candle Range Theory (CRT)** — the community-attributed framework popularized by Romeo / TTrades in 2024. ICT has addressed CRT publicly, and the position is consistent: **CRT is "based on my ideas but not my concept."** This file exists to make ICT's stance explicitly findable so the library's CRT entries can correctly attribute the framework as community-derived rather than ICT-original.

## Formal Criteria

ICT's position summarized:

- ICT acknowledges that CRT's underlying mechanic (range-bound sweep + reversal) is consistent with his teaching on Power-of-Three and liquidity sweeps.
- ICT does NOT claim CRT as his own concept.
- ICT does NOT formally endorse CRT as an extension of his framework.
- ICT credits the lineage: CRT-style mechanics derive from his 2016+ teaching, but the specific "Candle Range Theory" framing is Romeo / community-original.

## Common Mistakes

- **Citing ICT as the source of CRT.** Wrong; cite Romeo / TTrades.
- **Treating ICT's "based on my ideas" as endorsement.** It is acknowledgement of conceptual lineage, not endorsement of the specific CRT framework.
- **Conflating ICT's framework with CRT in mentorship discussions.** ICT teaches PO3, sweeps, OBs, FVGs; he does not teach CRT specifically.

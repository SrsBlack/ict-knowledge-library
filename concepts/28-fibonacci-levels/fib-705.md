## Definition

The 0.705 fib retracement is ICT's **canonical Optimal Trade Entry level** — the precise mid-point of the OTE zone (between 0.62 and 0.79). It is unusual in that it is **not a classical fibonacci ratio** (0.618 is the classical "golden ratio" retracement) — ICT specifically teaches 0.705 as the optimal algorithmic entry. The exact origin: 0.705 is the midpoint between 0.62 and 0.79, providing a balanced premium-or-discount entry at depth ~70%.

## Formal Criteria

- 0.705 = midpoint between 0.62 and 0.79 in the ICT retracement set.
- Functions as the highest-conviction OTE entry level when paired with a PD array.
- Often coincides with a previously-formed FVG, OB, or breaker on the retracement leg.

## Formula / Math

```
fib_705 = leg_end - 0.705 * leg_size

# Verify it's the midpoint of 0.62 and 0.79:
midpoint(0.62, 0.79) = (0.62 + 0.79) / 2 = 0.705 ✓

# Bullish leg from 1.0800 to 1.0900:
fib_705 = 1.0900 - 0.705 * 100 = 1.08295
```

**Example 1 — OTE 0.705 entry on bullish leg:**

- fib_705 = 1.08295.
- HTF bullish; bullish FVG at 1.0828–1.0832; price returns to 1.0830 (≈ 0.705).
- Long at 1.0830, SL below 0.79 + buffer = 1.0815, target -1.5 SD at 1.1050.
- Risk = 15, reward = 220 → ~14R setup at full extension.

## Common Mistakes

- **Demanding exact 0.705.** Use a small buffer (±0.5–1 pip on FX); pixel-precision misses real fills.
- **0.705 without PD array.** A "fib level" alone isn't entry; require a PD array (FVG/OB) at or near 0.705.
- **Confusing with 0.618.** Classical TA's 0.618 (golden ratio) is NOT ICT's 0.705. They differ by ~9%.

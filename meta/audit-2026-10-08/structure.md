## Cross-page issues (contradictions between pages, inconsistent numbers)

1. **Vocabulary layer is non-ICT across five pages.** mss, mss-vs-choch, bos-bullish, bos-bearish (and swing-high/low "Related" links) are built on CHoCH/BOS — 0 hits in 153 packets and 41 episodes.

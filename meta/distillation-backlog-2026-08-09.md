evidence about a lecture's subject only until you read it.**

## Method notes for whoever continues this

- **A lecture titled after a concept is strong evidence; a frequent phrase is weak.**
  The phrase scan rated `old high` (541 mentions) as the biggest gap in the corpus; it
  was an alias for four existing concepts.
- **Check for an existing sibling before writing.** Open float looked like a duplicate
  of the IPDA 60-day lookback and is not — IPDA is trailing, open float is symmetric
  with an expiry. Two near-misses of this kind have already been caught.
- **Watch for one word covering two mechanisms.** "Carrying charge" is a delivery-month
  curve in commodities and a rate carry in FX.
- **Do not bulk-generate.** Sixteen plausible-looking pages could be produced from these
  titles in one pass. They would pass lint and quietly contain fabrications, in a
  library whose entire value is that its lineage is checkable.
